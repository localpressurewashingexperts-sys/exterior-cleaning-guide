Exterior Cleaning and Property Maintenance Guide

Practical information for maintaining cleaner, healthier-looking property exteriors.

House Washing and Siding Care

Regular exterior inspections can help identify dirt, algae, mildew, and other surface concerns. Select cleaning methods based on the siding material and manufacturer recommendations.

Driveway and Concrete Cleaning

Concrete driveways can accumulate dirt, organic growth, and vehicle stains. Appropriate cleaning methods depend on the concrete condition, stain type, and surrounding landscaping.

Pressure Washing and Soft Washing

Pressure washing uses pressurized water, while soft washing generally relies on lower pressure and an appropriate cleaning solution. The right approach depends on the surface being cleaned.

Exterior Maintenance Checklist

  • Inspect siding and exterior walls for visible buildup.
  • Check driveways, patios, and walkways for stains.
  • Inspect decks before choosing a cleaning method.
  • Protect plants and nearby property during cleaning.
  • Follow product labels and appropriate safety guidance.
